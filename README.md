Flappi-Bird

Flappi-Bird is a Unity-powered clone of the classic "Flappy Bird," delivering the same addictive gameplay with modern enhancements. Players tap or click to guide a bird through endless pipes, avoiding obstacles to achieve the highest score.  

- **Classic Gameplay: Simple controls with increasing difficulty.  
- **Modern Design: Vibrant visuals, smooth animations, and dynamic effects.  
- **Customizations: Unlock bird skins, themed pipes, and power-ups.  
- **High Scores: Compete with friends and beat your personal best.  
- **Optimized Performance**: Runs smoothly on all devices.  

Flappi-Bird combines nostalgia with fresh elements, making it perfect for casual gamers and fans of retro arcade challenges.
